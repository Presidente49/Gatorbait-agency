# Meta Playbook — What Works (2026)

*Researched September 2026. Re-verify quarterly.*

## The One Rule
**Native post, never a bare link share.** Link-format posts get ~0.20% reach — the worst of all formats. Always attach a photo or video, put the article URL in the caption text.

## Format Priority
- **Facebook** (feed reach): Images 1.20% > Albums 1.15% > Status 0.80% > Reels 0.55% > Links 0.20%
- **Reels = discovery.** Shown to non-followers. Feed reaches followers; Reels find new ones. Do both.
- **Instagram:** Carousels 4.50% > Reels 4.10% > Images 4.00%

## Captions
- Hook in line 1-2. Short paragraphs, line breaks.
- Minimal caps, max 5 hashtags.
- Article post format: original hook + pasted excerpt (formatted) + article URL + attached image.

## Engagement (the algorithm's fuel)
- Reply to every comment **within the first hour**.
- Prompt shares and real comments, not just likes. Private shares are a top signal.
- Invite post reactors to follow the page.

## Cadence
- Page posts: 4-6/week is the studied optimum. **Game/event days are the exception — post freely.**
- Group shares: ~1/hour, the brand's niche groups only, share original Page posts (never re-uploads), vary the share text.

## A/B Testing
- Test stat-led hooks vs quote-led hooks; headline-overlay images vs clean photos.
- Track: reach, reactions, comments, shares, link clicks per variant.
- Winner becomes the new default. Report it like an agency: what won, by how much, what's next.

## What Kills Reach
Bare link shares · reposted clips (Meta fingerprints video) · watermarks · clickbait · 7+ page posts/day (non-game-day)
