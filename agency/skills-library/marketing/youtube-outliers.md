# YouTube Outliers — Steal What's Proven, Package It Better

**Primary owner:** scout (runs the analysis) → hype (publishes the package). Critical for any brand rebuilding or scaling a YouTube channel. Concepts only — no code vendored.

## The outlier rule

An **outlier** is any video performing **2x+ its channel's average views**. Study everything above that line — it holds the packaging patterns (titles, thumbnails, hooks) that the algorithm already validated.

## How to run an analysis

1. Pick 5–10 channels in the niche (competitors + adjacent creators).
2. Pull the last 30–90 days of uploads with views.
3. Flag outliers (2x+ channel average). Extract: title wording, thumbnail composition, hook style, length, topic bucket, publish cadence.
4. Extract **title patterns** — common words/phrases across outliers indicate proven formats.

## Proven packaging skeletons (adapt, don't clone)

- "X, Clearly Explained"
- "X Hours of Y in Z Minutes"
- "The Laziest Way to X"
- "[Number] [Things] That [Outcome]"
- "I [Did Hard Thing] So You Don't Have To"
- "The Truth About [Topic Everyone Gets Wrong]"
- Question-led: "Why Does [Surprising Thing] Happen?"

Skeletons are structures, not scripts — the content inside must be genuinely good or the packaging backfires.

## Before publishing (the pre-flight)

1. Pull the brand channel's own baseline: by topic, title pattern, thumbnail pattern, length, publish day/time, format.
2. Check impressions, CTR, average view duration, retention curve, watch time, subscribers gained, comments, traffic source.
3. Compare the proposed title/thumbnail/hook against similar historical videos AND competitor outliers.
4. **Do not call packaging validated until analytics are checked.** Otherwise it's a screenplay wearing a lab coat.

## After publishing (readback windows)

- **24–48 hours:** CTR and early retention — the thumbnail/title verdict.
- **7 days:** average view duration and watch time — the content verdict.
- **28 days:** topic durability and subscriber gain — the strategy verdict.

Log per video: video ID, title, thumbnail concept, hook, topic bucket, retention devices, publish date. Then run the readback (`ANALYTICS-LOOP.md`): promote the pattern to the playbook only if the candidate beats baseline.

## Testing a new package on a live video

Swapping titles or thumbnails on a live video is an experiment: follow `agency/growth/PACKAGING-TESTS.md` (control arm, minimum volume per arm, 95% z-test, retention guardrail, separate yes to adopt). At 1,000 impressions per arm, a 5% vs 4% CTR gap is noise (z = 1.08).

## Performance learning rule

When a title/thumbnail package wins, the win belongs to the **pattern**, not the video: "stat-led question titles + 3-word thumbnails beat narrative titles on this channel." Log the pattern in the brand's `learnings.md` with the numbers that proved it — that's what compounds across videos and across brands.

## Ethics and platform rules

- Adapt formats; never clone thumbnails, copy titles verbatim, or impersonate creators.
- No misleading packaging — the content must deliver what the title promises, or retention dies and the channel pays for it long-term.
