# Offer Design — The Thing Underneath the Copy

**Primary owner:** bridge (client-facing offers, sponsorships, partnerships) + scribe (offer language). Applies to agency retainers, merch drops, memberships, and any direct-response promotion. Concepts only — no code vendored.

## Core philosophy

**The offer is the thing, not the page.** Better copy on a weak offer compounds slowly; a stronger offer with average copy converts immediately. When someone asks for "better copy," check the offer first — most weak-copy requests are weak-offer requests in disguise.

## The Value Equation

```
              Dream Outcome  ×  Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
  Value  =  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Time Delay     ×   Effort & Sacrifice
```

| Lever | Meaning | How to move it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dream outcome ↑** | What the customer actually wants | Name the bigger goal behind the surface ask. Be specific. |
| **Perceived likelihood ↑** | Do they believe they'll get it | Proof (case studies, named customers, data), guarantees, methodology specificity |
| **Time delay ↓** | How long until the result | Faster onboarding, faster first win, shorter end-to-end timeline |
| **Effort & sacrifice ↓** | What it costs them beyond money (time, work, risk) | Done-for-you, simpler process, fewer decisions, lower learning curve |

Most "lower the price" requests are actually "raise the numerator or lower the denominator" requests. Price is the comparison, not the value.

## The six components of a complete offer

| # | Component | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Core deliverable** | What do they get? |
| 2 | **Bonus stack** | What else makes the core feel undervalued? |
| 3 | **Guarantee** | What happens if it doesn't work? |
| 4 | **Scarcity / urgency** | Why now, not later? |
| 5 | **Name** | What is this thing called? |
| 6 | **Price + payment structure** | What do they pay, and how? |

Most weak offers fail on bonuses (none), guarantees (none or the wrong type), or scarcity (none, or fake). Most cringe offers fail on fake scarcity or over-promised guarantees.

**Bonuses:** additive, not inflated. Stacking "$50K of bonuses" on a cheap product reads as scam to sophisticated buyers.
**Guarantees:** pick the right type for the business model — conditional beats unconditional for services; unconditional eats margin.
**Scarcity:** real only. Real inventory limits, real enrollment windows, real capacity. Fake countdown timers buy a short-term lift and a permanent trust collapse.
**Payment structure:** plans and down-sells beat discounts. Never discount to acquire — discount-*askers* churn at ~2× the rate of full-price customers, and a coupon anchors the product as cheap. Raise value with the offer instead.

## The diagnostic loop (when an offer isn't converting)

1. **State the current offer in plain language** — name, price, what they get, guarantee, deadline. Write it down even if it lives in scattered places.
2. **Score the Value Equation** — rate each of the four levers 1–10. The lowest is the binding constraint.
3. **Audit the six components** — which is missing or weak?
4. **Fix one lever this iteration.** Don't rebuild everything. The biggest lever is usually the lowest score.
5. **Project the lift honestly.** Most single-component changes deliver 10–40% conversion lift. Two consecutive iterations on different levers can stack to 2–3×. Anyone promising 5x is selling something.

## Banned vocabulary in offer language

"Game-changing", "revolutionary", "disruptive", "next-level", "10x", "secret", "hidden", "limited time" with no real limit, inflated "$X value" with no comparable, "100% guaranteed" without conditions. Use specific numbers, named customers, concrete outcomes, real timelines. Specificity beats superlatives.

## Agency notes

- **Retainers:** the deliverable is the outcome (pipeline, revenue), not the activity list. Name it. Guarantee response/schedule, not revenue you can't control.
- **Merch:** scarcity is real inventory + real drop windows. Bonuses: bundles and free-shipping thresholds. Price anchoring: show the "fan" vs. "pro" tiers.
- **Cross-brand:** a winning offer anatomy in one brand is a template for others — log the pattern in the brand's learnings and the agency playbook (`ANALYTICS-LOOP.md` readback first).
