# Audience — Gator Bait Media

- **Who:** Florida Gators fans — alumni, students, Gainesville locals,
  national Gator diaspora. Skews 25–55, heavily male but growing female
  audience via reels.
- **Care about:** Winning, players as people, insider access, being
  right about the program before national media catches on.
- **Pain points:** Years of losing (four losing seasons in five before
  2026); national disrespect; rival fans (Tennessee, Georgia, FSU).
- **Hang out:** Facebook (GatorBaitMedia.com group ~3.7K, Gator Nation
  Kingdom ~9.1K, big outside groups like Gator Nation ~107K),
  Instagram, YouTube (The Buddy Martin Show).
- **Buy/share/follow triggers:** Big wins, mic'd-up coach moments,
  player milestones, "we're back" narratives, playful destruction of
  rivals.
