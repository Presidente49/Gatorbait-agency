# Brand Style — Gator Bait Media

*Visual identity source of truth. Owner: Blueprint.*

## Colors
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Gator Orange | #FA4616 | Headlines, CTAs, accents |
| Gator Blue | #0021A5 | Backgrounds, secondary |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on dark, clean backgrounds |
| Black | #000000 | Text on light |

## Typography
| Role | Font | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Headlines | **Anton** (Google Fonts, free) | All-caps, bold, condensed. Every graphic headline. |
| Body/captions | System/natural | Keep it readable, don't over-style |

## Image Specs (Meta 2026)
| Use | Size | Ratio |
|-----|------|-------|
| Feed photo | 1080x1350 | 4:5 |
| Reel / Story / TikTok | 1080x1920 | 9:16 |
| Carousel card | 1080x1080 | 1:1 |

## Graphic Rules
- **Vertical beats everything.** Never post 16:9 landscape in the feed.
- Big headline, few words. Must read on a phone at a glance.
- Keep text/logos out of top/bottom 250px on vertical video (Meta overlays cover it).
- ~80px safe margin on feed images; corners may round.
- Real photos beat polished graphics. Authentic > studio.
- Minimal text on images still reaches further (the old 20% rule is gone, but less is more).

## Video Rules
- Hook in the first 2-3 seconds.
- Burned-in captions (85% watch muted).
- Reels: 15-30s. Never open with a logo.
